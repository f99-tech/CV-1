# SIGNLOCK

Play the whole game in the browser. No Vercel. No extra apps. No signup.

**[Open SIGNLOCK](https://f99-tech.github.io/CV-1/)**

https://f99-tech.github.io/CV-1/

On your phone: open that link in Safari or Chrome → Allow camera → play.

If GitHub or anyone asks to “import / upload CV-1 to Vercel”, skip it. The game is already live at the link above.

## How to play

1. Tap to enter (unlocks sound).
2. Pick best of 3 / 5 / 7 / 9.
3. Allow the camera, palm facing the lens.
4. Tap **Lock in**. On **SHOOT**, hold rock (fist), paper (open palm), or scissors (V).
5. No camera? Use the three throw buttons.

Video stays on your phone. The bot cannot peek at your throw.
