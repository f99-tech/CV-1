# SIGNLOCK

Play in the browser. The **camera** reads your hand. No Vercel. No sign buttons.

**[Open SIGNLOCK](https://f99-tech.github.io/CV-1/)**

https://f99-tech.github.io/CV-1/

## How to play

1. Open the link on your phone.
2. Allow the camera.
3. Tap **Lock in**, then hold the sign to the lens on **SHOOT**.
4. Fist = rock. Open palm = paper. V = scissors.

The throw is locked by computer vision, not by tapping a sign.
